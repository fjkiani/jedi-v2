# JEDI Components - Small Business Focus

## Overview
The JEDI components have been completely restructured to focus on small businesses as the target audience. All technical jargon has been removed and replaced with simple, business-focused language that small business owners can easily understand.

## Key Changes Made

### 1. **Component Names & Descriptions**
- **JEDI Ensemble™**: "Multi-Model AI Orchestration" → "Smart AI Assistant"
- **JEDI Rules™**: "Business Logic Engine" → "Business Automation Assistant"  
- **JEDI Automate™**: "Automated Model Optimization" → "AI Performance Booster"

### 2. **Problem Statements**
- **Before**: Technical problems like "AI Model Complexity & Selection"
- **After**: Small business problems like "AI Confusion & Overwhelm"

### 3. **Capabilities**
- **Before**: Technical features like "Intelligent Model Selection"
- **After**: Business benefits like "Picks the Right AI Tool"

### 4. **Language Changes**
- **Before**: "Automatically selects the best AI model for each specific task"
- **After**: "Automatically chooses the best AI tool for each job"

### 5. **User Benefits**
- **Before**: "No need to research or compare models"
- **After**: "Just tell us what you need - we'll pick the perfect AI tool for the job"

### 6. **Business Value**
- **Before**: Technical complexity hidden from user
- **After**: Clear business value like "Saves you hours of research and prevents costly mistakes"

## Content Strategy

### **Always Emphasize**:
- **Works immediately** - no setup or technical knowledge needed
- **Saves time and money** - automates tasks that waste your time
- **Grows with your business** - scales automatically as you grow
- **Real small business results** - actual implementations from businesses like yours
- **Simple and clear** - easy to understand and use

### **Content Structure**:
1. **Small Business Problem** - Real challenge faced by small business owners
2. **JEDI Solution** - How JEDI makes it simple and automatic
3. **What You Get** - Clear benefits without technical jargon
4. **Real Results** - Actual small business success stories with numbers
5. **Works for Any Business** - How it applies to different types of businesses

## Example Transformations

### **Before (Technical)**:
```
"Intelligent Model Selection - Automatically selects the best AI model for each specific task based on context, performance requirements, and cost constraints."
```

### **After (Small Business)**:
```
"Picks the Right AI Tool - Automatically chooses the best AI tool for each job, so you don't have to research or compare different options."
```

### **Before (Technical)**:
```
"Natural Language Rule Definition - Define business rules in plain English - JEDI Rules converts them into executable logic automatically."
```

### **After (Small Business)**:
```
"Write Rules in Plain English - Just tell us your business rules in simple language - we'll turn them into automated actions."
```
