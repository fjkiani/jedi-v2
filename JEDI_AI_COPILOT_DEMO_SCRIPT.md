## 🎪 **Demo Tips & Best Practices**

### **Before the Demo:**
1. **Test Everything:** Run through the entire demo flow
2. **Prepare Backup:** Have screenshots ready in case of technical issues
3. **Customize Examples:** Use industry-relevant examples for your audience
4. **Check Data:** Ensure Hygraph CMS has fresh, relevant content

### **During the Demo:**
1. **Engage the Audience:** Ask questions about their specific challenges
2. **Highlight Differentiation:** Constantly compare to generic AI assistants
3. **Show Real Data:** Emphasize that everything is using actual, current data
4. **Encourage Interaction:** Let them suggest queries to try

### **After the Demo:**
1. **Capture Feedback:** Note what resonated most
2. **Schedule Follow-up:** While interest is high
3. **Provide Resources:** Share relevant case studies or technical documentation
4. **Track Engagement:** Monitor which features generated the most interest
